# Awesome Creator Economy [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of platforms, tools, and resources for sellers, creators, and buyers in the digital economy.

The creator economy is fragmented across dozens of tools — checkout platforms, UGC marketplaces, affiliate networks, analytics dashboards, and more. This list gathers the best of them in one place, so makers can ship faster and creators can earn more.

## Platforms to Sell Digital Products

Tools for selling downloadable products, templates, ebooks, and digital assets.

- [Peddlum](https://peddlum.com) - Digital product marketplace where sellers keep 90% with built-in UGC creator campaigns and affiliate tracking.
- [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com) - Simple checkout platform for selling digital downloads and memberships.
- [Lemon Squeezy](https://lemonsqueezy.com) - Payments and subscriptions platform built for SaaS and digital products.
- [Payhip](https://payhip.com) - Sell digital downloads, courses, and memberships with low fees.
- [Sellfy](https://sellfy.com) - Storefront builder for digital products and print-on-demand.
- [SendOwl](https://sendowl.com) - Digital delivery and checkout for creators.

- [Stan Store](https://stan.store) - Link-in-bio store for selling digital products to social audiences.
- [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com) - Support page and shop for creators with no platform fees.

- [Widest Life](https://widest.life) - All-in-one super-app platform with 70+ apps covering every life stage from birth to end of life.

## Contributing

Contributions are welcome. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for guidelines.

To suggest a new entry:

1. Fork this repository.
2. Add your entry to the appropriate section in alphabetical order, except for sections explicitly noted otherwise.
3. Use this format: `- [Name](URL) - One-sentence description ending with a period.`
4. Open a pull request with a brief explanation of why the entry belongs.

We accept entries that are actively maintained, useful to creators, sellers, or buyers, and offer real value beyond marketing.
